**First Author** (Representative Work)

Riemannian Fuzzy K-Means: Answered the Open Problem of manifold clustering. Existing Riemannian K-Means algorithms require alternating iterative calculation of assignment and Frechet center. While computing assignment is the same as Euclidean space time, calculating the Frechet center requires Riemannian optimization algorithms, i.e., solving via For loops, with a time complexity of O(νω). The RFK algorithm relaxes Riemannian K-Means and converts the whole problem into a single Riemannian optimization problem, with a time complexity of O(ν). The experimental part achieves over 100 times acceleration on some datasets. Evaluated by a relevant domain Fellow as "It overcomes the significant computational hurdles".
